# E! Support prototype

Clickable prototype of the Support feature in E!: bug reports with scripted AI chat help, Needs Action, Reviewer questions and status changes.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or view the published artifact. Everything runs in the page: no logins, no database, nothing is saved, and the AI replies are scripted. The data is fictional.

Prototype controls are in the dashed strip at the top: Switch role (Submitter, Account Admin, Colleague, Reviewer), Language (English, German), AI unavailable, Journeys (starts journeys A, B and C1–C4 in the right role) and Reset data.
